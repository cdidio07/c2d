The Opportunity

The Mission Is Powerful. The Model Must Be Stronger.

Performing arts organizations create extraordinary artistic, educational, cultural, and community value. Yet many remain dependent on annual fundraising, episodic campaigns, fragile attendance patterns, and operating models that were not designed for the environment they now face.

The answer is not to abandon philanthropy or commercialize the artistic mission. It is to build a stronger enterprise around that mission. C2D Partnerships helps organizations determine what should be preserved, what must be fixed, what can be scaled, and where investment can create lasting institutional value.

Not a checklist to work through. A system built around the mission.

01

Preserve What Works

Protect the artistic, cultural, and institutional strengths that make the organization distinctive.

02

Fix What Does Not

Address structural weaknesses, fragmented execution, declining loyalty, and unsustainable practices.

03

Scale What Creates Impact

Expand the programs, relationships, and revenue engines capable of creating both mission and financial value.

04

Invest in Talent

Develop the people, leadership, artists, educators, and institutional capabilities required for the future.

The objective is not simply organizational survival. It is institutional strength, relevance, and lasting impact.

From First Experience to Lifelong Advocacy

Every performance, class, community program, partnership, and digital interaction is an opportunity to begin or strengthen a relationship.

High-performing arts organizations do not manage education, audiences, fundraising, community engagement, and advocacy as disconnected activities. They create an integrated journey that moves people from participation to belonging, and from belonging to long-term support.

About the Founder

An Uncommon Perspective on Institutional Leadership

Executive · Strategist · Performing Arts Leader · Advisor

C2D Partnerships was founded by Carlo Di Dio, whose career has unfolded across two worlds that rarely meet: the artistic world of performance and the strategic world of organizational transformation.

Born in Italy and later building his career in the United States, Carlo spent years as an award-winning professional ballet dancer. That experience gave him a firsthand understanding of the discipline, creativity, collaboration, vulnerability, and extraordinary human effort behind artistic excellence.

His career later expanded into business transformation, strategic program leadership, partnerships, and consulting. In his current role in strategic managed services at PwC, Carlo leads complex initiatives requiring executive alignment, cross-functional coordination, financial accountability, and disciplined execution.

His nonprofit board service with Baryshnikov Arts Center and New Jersey Ballet has given him direct experience with governance, strategy, financial stewardship, audience development, partnerships, and the institutional responsibilities of performing arts leadership.

Artistic excellence and business discipline are not opposing forces. Each makes the other more sustainable.

His work is dedicated to helping performing arts organizations protect their missions while developing the enterprise capabilities required to serve future generations.

Read the Founder’s Complete Biography

Dancer

Carlo trained at the San Carlo Theatre Ballet School in Naples under Anna Razzi, former étoile of La Scala, and later joined the Junior Ballet at the École Supérieure de Danse Rosella Hightower in Cannes. As a professional dancer and guest artist from 2003 to 2018, he performed on the stages of the Teatro Massimo of Palermo, the Teatro San Carlo of Naples, the San Diego Opera, California Ballet, Tulsa Opera, the Cairo Opera House, and in productions in New York City, dancing principal and soloist roles in Giselle, Carmen, Romeo and Juliet, Swan Lake, Don Quixote, Le Corsaire, Dracula, Schéhérazade, Aida, and Samson and Delilah, among others. He shared the stage with artists including Roberto Bolle, Carla Fracci, and Leonid Sarafanov, and in 2012 received the Danza & Danza Prize as Best Italian Dancer Abroad, following Talent and Career Recognition awards at the Loano Dance Festival in 2010 and 2011.

Educator and Program Builder

Carlo co-founded and served as Executive Director of the Torres Di Dio Ballet Program in San Diego, leading enrollment, budgeting, staffing, and community engagement while training a new generation of dancers — several of whom went on to institutions including the Colorado Ballet Academy, the Rock School for Dance Education, and the Kansas City Ballet School.

Strategy and Transformation

Carlo holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from San Diego State University and an MBA in Project Management from Louisiana State University, and is continuing his education in arts management at New York University. He is a certified PMP®, Certified Scrum Master, and SAFe® 6 practitioner. He began his transformation career at AMN Healthcare, then led project and program management engagements for U.S. Navy medical programs at Booz Allen Hamilton. He currently leads enterprise transformation and strategic alliance initiatives in PwC’s Strategic Managed Services practice in New York.

Board Service

Since 2025, Carlo has served on the boards of the Baryshnikov Arts Center and the New Jersey Ballet, contributing to development planning, donor engagement, and strategy and marketing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What types of organizations does C2D Partnerships advise?

Our advisory focus includes ballet and dance companies, opera companies, orchestras, theaters, performing arts centers, cultural institutions, conservatories, academies, and arts-education organizations.

Does C2D Partnerships provide traditional fundraising consulting?

Our work may include revenue diversification, partnerships, campaign-to-sustainability planning, and relationship development, but the practice is not positioned as a traditional fundraising consultancy. The focus is on building stronger enterprise models around the artistic mission.

Can C2D Partnerships work alongside an existing strategic plan?

Yes. Many organizations already know where they want to go but need stronger prioritization, governance, coordination, performance measurement, or implementation support.

Does C2D Partnerships work with boards as well as management?

Yes. Engagements may involve executive leadership, artistic leadership, boards, committees, and cross-functional teams, depending on the organization’s needs.

Are engagements limited to organizations in the United States?

No. Our founder’s international background and cultural network support selected international partnerships, exchanges, initiatives, and institutional collaborations.

How does an engagement begin?

It begins with a focused conversation about the organization’s priorities, challenges, context, and desired outcomes. The appropriate scope is then developed based on the organization’s specific needs.
